Common questions

How do I sign in?

Use your @umass.edu email. We’ll send a one-time code — no password.

I was charged but didn’t get food

Email us with your order time and building. Include the email on the account. We’ll look up the payment and help resolve it.

How do runners get paid?

Link a bank account in Earnings (Stripe Connect), complete deliveries, then tap Cash out. Payouts go to your linked debit/bank.

Can I delete my account?

Email support@dormrun.app from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We’ll remove your profile and associated data.

Safety

Meet in public dorm / building areas. Never share your handoff code in chat. Report issues to support right away.

For App Review

DormRun requires a UMass email OTP to sign in. If you need a demo path for review, contact us at the email above and we’ll provide test credentials / OTP assistance.
