Privacy Policy

How DormRun collects, uses, and shares information when you use our campus delivery app.

Last updated: November 21, 2026 · Contact: support@dormrun.app

1. Who we are

DormRun (“we”, “us”) is a campus food delivery app for students. This policy covers the iOS app and related support pages. First campus: UMass Amherst.

2. Information we collect

  • Account: school email address, name, phone (if provided), home building, profile photo.
  • Orders: dining location, items, drop-off building/notes, tips, fees, order status, chat messages.
  • Location: runner location during active deliveries (to show live tracking to the customer).
  • Payments: payment processing via Stripe (card/Apple Pay). We do not store full card numbers.
  • Runner payouts: Stripe Connect account identifiers needed to send earnings to your bank.
  • Device: push notification token, basic app diagnostics needed to operate the service.

3. How we use information

  • Create and manage your account (OTP sign-in with your .edu email).
  • Place, fulfill, track, and support deliveries.
  • Process payments and runner cash-outs.
  • Send order status notifications and Live Activity updates.
  • Improve reliability and prevent fraud/abuse.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with law.

4. How we share information

  • Other users on an order: customers and assigned runners see what’s needed to complete delivery (e.g. first name, building, status, chat, runner location while en route).
  • Stripe: payment and payout processing.
  • Supabase / hosting providers: authentication, database, and backend services that power the app.
  • Legal: if required by law or to protect users and the service.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Location

Location is used for delivery tracking. Runners share location while a delivery is active. You can disable location permission in iOS Settings; some runner features won’t work without it.

6. Retention

We keep account and order records as long as needed to operate the service, handle disputes, and meet legal requirements. You may request deletion (see below).

7. Your choices

  • Update profile details in the app.
  • Control notification and location permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Request account deletion by emailing support@dormrun.app from your account email.

8. Children

DormRun is intended for college students and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards (encrypted transport, access controls). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. We’ll post the new version on this page and update the “Last updated” date.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: support@dormrun.app
